An interactive platform designed to uncover geological and environmental parallels between Earth's extreme terrains and target landing sites on the Moon and Mars—supporting future space missions and analog research bases.

Platform Capabilities

Tools built for planetary researchers, students, and mission planners

3D Terrains

Interact with and generate elevation-accurate 3D terrain models from planetary datasets to inspect craters, lava tubes, and regolith analogs.

Interactive Globe

Navigate an interactive globe to compare terrestrial analog sites directly against lunar and Martian coordinates using multi-spectral data similarities.

Custom Research

Upload your own geological or environmental datasets as custom inputs to run similarity scoring and discover new analog candidates.
